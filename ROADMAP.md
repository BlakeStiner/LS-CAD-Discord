# Lakeside Medical CAD Roadmap

## Before live-server rollout

- Configurable departments and rosters: server-managed roster names, role access, colors, and supervisor assignments. The current clock-in selector remains intentionally fixed for development testing.
- Administrative shift workflow: manual entries, corrections, approval/rejection, and audit history.

## Planned operational features

- Coverage and staffing alerts: roster-specific minimum staffing, leadership alerts when coverage falls below a threshold, and optional scheduled-shift reminders.
- Shift handoff notes and coverage requests.
- Reporting exports for leadership review.
